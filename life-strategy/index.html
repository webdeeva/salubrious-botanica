MEET YOUR LIFE STRATEGIST

I am a Life Strategist for high-achieving women who are ready to reconnect with themselves, reclaim their inner authority, and create lives that feel aligned from the inside out.

This work is a natural evolution of my background as a therapist, but it moves beyond traditional 1:1 therapy. Life strategy allows me to support women not only with what they are healing from, but with what they are becoming.

My work blends emotional insight, practical strategy, spiritual awareness, and grounded accountability. I help women identify the patterns, fears, obligations, and old identities that keep them stuck, so they can make clearer decisions in their relationships, careers, purpose, and personal lives.

This path also gives me the freedom to serve in a more expansive way: through virtual work, travel, retreats, teaching, and transformational experiences that are not limited to one office or one traditional model.

T. Nicole Ellis

WHAT TO KNOW

A reflective, strategic space.

What is life strategy?

Life strategy is a non-clinical service for personal growth, clarity, alignment, and intentional living. It provides a reflective and strategic space to explore patterns, strengthen self-trust, clarify direction, and reconnect with your inner authority. The work blends emotional insight, practical guidance, intuitive reflection, and grounded accountability for those ready to move through life with more clarity and purpose.

Is this therapy?

No. Unlike therapy, life strategy does not diagnose or treat mental health concerns. If you are looking for clinical care, our sister practice, Salubrious Therapy Solutions, offers therapy.

How is it offered?

Sessions are virtual, so you can work with Nicole wherever you are. Retreats, teaching, and transformational experiences are offered here as they are announced.

Do you offer retreats?

Yes. Retreats are part of this work and will be announced here and in the newsletter. Contact us to hear about upcoming dates.
