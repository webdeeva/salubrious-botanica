A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your ritual, made simple.

Is life strategy the same as therapy?

No. Life strategy is a non-clinical service for personal growth, clarity, alignment, and intentional living. It does not diagnose or treat mental health concerns. If you are looking for therapy, our sister practice, Salubrious Therapy Solutions, can help.

Is Salubrious Botanica online only?

Yes. Salubrious Botanica is an online wellness store that ships within the United States. Contact us for current product availability and fulfillment details.

How can I learn more about a product?

Email T.Nicole@salubriousbotanica.com or call 757-593-2496 for product questions.

Are herbs and elixirs medical treatments?

Products are intended for general wellness support. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.
